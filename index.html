Identified Problem: Poor Venue Management & Lack of Course Guidelines

Where: Classrooms/venues during start of new semester | Who is affected: Students and Lecturers at ZUT

What currently happens: Classes get canceled last minute because no venue was pre-booked, or venue is too small. Students start courses without clear outline, learning outcomes, roadmap. No central place for study materials, past papers, schedules.

Why it needs addressing: Canceled classes waste time, disrupt learning, low attendance, poor grades. Students can't prepare ahead. Study efficiency drops.

Problem Domain

Where?

Across campus venues at the start of this semester, students struggle to find lecture locations.

Who?

New and returning Students and Lecturers affected by last-minute changes.

What?

Frequent cancellations, venues too small, and no clear outline of lecture schedules.

Why?

Leads to wasted time, missed lectures, and low grades.

Proposed System: DG Library and Venue Hub

System Name: DG Library and Venue Hub | Purpose: Centralized smart venue & learning resource hub | Intended Users: Students & Lecturers | Benefits: Stops cancellations, clear study roadmap, centralized materials, improved communication

1. Smart Timetable & Venue Manager

Admin dashboard shows available venues, auto-allocates rooms by class size, notifies students/lecturers of changes instantly.

2. Course Roadmap & Resource Hub

Each course has outline, learning outcomes, progress bar, downloadable notes, past papers, and notepad.

3. Messaging & File Sharing

Direct communication between students and lecturers, secure uploads/downloads for materials, role-based access.
