DG Library and Venue Hub - Solving School Venue & Resource Crisis

Meet Our 10-Member Team GitHub Repo

Identified Problem: Poor Venue Management & Lack of Course Guidelines

Where: Classrooms/venues during start of new semester

Who is affected: Students and Lecturers at Zambia University College of Technology(ZUT)

What currently happens: Classes get canceled last minute because no venue was pre-booked, or venue is too small. Students start courses without clear outline, learning outcomes, roadmap. No central place for study materials, past papers, schedules.

Why it needs addressing: Canceled classes waste time, disrupt learning, low attendance, poor grades. Students can't prepare ahead. Study efficiency drops.

SYSTEM FEATURES

Features & Benefits

DG Library and Venue Hub provides practical tools designed to improve venue coordination, course preparation, communication, and access to learning resources.

01
📅

Smart Timetable & Venue Manager

A centralized timetable and venue management feature that helps administrators allocate suitable venues and keep students and lecturers informed about schedule changes.

Key Capabilities

  • View venue availability
  • Allocate rooms based on class size
  • Manage class timetables
  • Notify users about schedule changes
Benefit: Reduces class cancellations, improves planning, and makes better use of available venues.
02
📚

Course Roadmap & Resource Hub

A centralized learning space where students can understand their courses, follow their learning progress, and access important academic resources.

Key Capabilities

  • Course outlines and learning outcomes
  • Learning roadmap and progress tracking
  • Access to notes and study materials
  • Past papers and revision resources
Benefit: Gives students a clear learning direction and makes academic resources easier to find.
03
💬

Messaging & File Sharing

A communication and resource-sharing feature that connects students and lecturers while providing controlled access to course-related files.

Key Capabilities

  • Student-to-lecturer communication
  • Course-related messaging
  • Upload and download learning materials
  • Role-based access to shared resources
Benefit: Improves communication, centralizes learning materials, and supports collaboration.
RISK MANAGEMENT

Risks & Mitigations

Potential implementation risks are identified early so the system can remain reliable, secure, and easy to use.

⚠️ Incorrect Timetable Data

Mitigation: Use data validation and administrator approval before timetable changes are published.

🔐 Unauthorized Access

Mitigation: Apply role-based permissions for students, lecturers, and administrators.

💾 File or Resource Loss

Mitigation: Maintain regular backups of important academic resources and system data.

👥 Poor User Adoption

Mitigation: Provide a simple interface, clear navigation, and basic user guidance.

🌐 Connectivity Challenges

Mitigation: Keep the interface lightweight, responsive, and optimized for different connection speeds.
